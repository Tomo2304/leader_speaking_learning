*6-month plan | Start date: 2026-04-24 | Target: 2026-10-23*
*Default: 15 min/day | ⏱ 30-min days: max 2/week, Tue–Thu only | Mon & Fri always 15 min*

---

## Daily Rhythm (every week, every day)

| Time | Activity | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Commute (train) | Passive learning — listen, read, or PREP drill on phone | 15–30 min |
| Home (evening) | Active practice — writing, recording, or structured exercise | 15–30 min |
| Weekend (one session) | Shadowing — speak along with a leader clip | 20–30 min |

---

## Weekly Rhythm

Every week follows this pattern:
- **One primary focus skill** — you are working on one thing at a time, not everything
- **Vocabulary bank** — any better phrasing you find or I find during review gets added automatically
- **Weekly check-in with Claude** — 10–15 min to report, get assessed, and set focus for next week
