## How to do Claude practice tasks (📼 tasks)

Tasks marked with **📼** are spoken practice with Claude — pressure simulations, PREP mock sessions, challenge handling roleplays, and meeting facilitation roleplays.

Use this hybrid method for every 📼 task:

1. **Hit record** on your phone's voice memo app *before* you start.
2. **Speak your answer aloud first** — this is the real practice (pace, pause, intonation, fillers).
3. **Then type or dictate** what you said to Claude. Claude reads only text — it assesses *content* (PREP structure, hedging, vocabulary, composure in word choice), not delivery.
4. **Play back the recording later** — judge delivery yourself, or run it through Orai for filler-word and pace feedback.

**Why both?** Claude Code can't transcribe audio files. Voice dictation in Claude only converts speech to text — Claude still sees just the words, not how you said them. So you record for delivery feedback (Orai / self-review), and type for content feedback (Claude). One pass, both signals.

> 📼 **Reminder:** when you see the 📼 icon in your daily Telegram brief, this is the method to use. If you forget, scroll back to this section.

## Reading approach — be realistic

You have ~2.25 hours of at-home book time + commute reading across 26 weeks. That's not enough to finish 5 books cover-to-cover. Use this tier system instead:

- **Read fully (highest ROI):** *The Pyramid Principle* (Minto) and *Think Faster, Talk Smarter* (Abrahams). These directly target your two biggest gaps — bottom-up structure and spontaneous speaking.
- **Read selectively (extract & apply):** *Exactly What to Say* (Jones) and *Crucial Conversations* (Patterson et al.). Treat these as reference books — the Tuesday tasks tell you exactly which chapters or themes to extract. Don't feel guilty skipping the rest.
- **Optional / skip:** *Supercommunicators* (Duhigg) was originally on the schedule for Weeks 7 and 10 — those slots are now podcast sessions instead. Read this book only if you finish the priority two with time to spare.

**Goal:** finish 2 books fully, extract from 2 more. That's a strong 6-month outcome, not a weakness.
