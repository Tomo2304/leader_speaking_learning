# Question Bank — Senior-Leader-Style Questions

*Use this bank for unprepared PREP practice. The goal is to answer questions you haven't pre-thought — so pick ones you haven't used recently.*

*Referenced from: Week 5 Tue, Week 6 Mon, Week 7 Mon, Week 21 Wed, and any "PREP mock" or pressure-practice task.*

---

## How to use

- Pick 3 questions per session.
- Choose ones you **haven't used in the last 2 weeks** — the value is in unprepared response, not rehearsal.
- Don't think for more than 10 seconds before speaking.
- After each answer, mark the question with the date you used it (so you don't repeat too soon).

---

## A. Project & Strategy

1. What's the ROI of your current project — in one sentence?
2. If you had to cut one initiative in your team's roadmap tomorrow, which one and why?
3. Why are you prioritising your data pipeline work over the dashboard redesign? (or whatever your current trade-off is)
4. What's the one decision you've made this quarter that you'd defend hardest?
5. If your project shipped 3 months late, what would the business impact be?
6. What does success for your AI Steering Committee contributions look like in 6 months?
7. How does your current work map to the company's top strategic priority?

## B. Risk, Blockers & Trade-offs

8. Where is your team falling behind — and what would it take to catch up?
9. What's the biggest risk in your current work that you haven't surfaced to leadership?
10. What's one thing your team is doing that you suspect doesn't matter?
11. If I gave you one extra senior engineer for 3 months, where would you put them?
12. What dependency are you most exposed to right now?
13. What would you stop doing if you had no political cost?

## C. Leadership & Self-reflection

14. What's your team's biggest weakness — and what are you doing about it?
15. What would you do differently if you started this year over?
16. Where do you add the most unique value as a leader?
17. What's a recent piece of feedback you received that stung — and what did you do with it?
18. Who on your team is most underutilised, and why?
19. If you were promoted tomorrow, who would replace you — and why aren't they ready yet?
20. What's one thing you believe about data leadership that most of your peers would disagree with?
