# Leadership Effective Communication — Project Overview

*Owner: Tomo | Started: 2026-04-24 | Target: 2 quarters (by ~2026-10-30)*

---

## Goal

To communicate confidently like capable native English-speaking leaders — using the right vocabulary, structure, and delivery to appear confident, positive, and well-thought, and to gain trust from others through the way I communicate.

## Key Contexts Where This Matters Most
- AI Steering Committee (enterprise-wide)
- Enterprise strategic project (representative / lead)
- Senior leader and executive interactions
- Running meetings with cross-functional teams
- Unprepared moments when asked for opinions on the spot

## Background
- Japanese-born, moved to NZ at 21, now based in Melbourne
- Technical Data Analytics Lead — communicates with IT, business teams, senior leaders, executives
- 18 years speaking English at work, comfortable (8/10) but communication not as sophisticated as it could be
- Core habit to fix: bottom-up communication (context first, conclusion last) — classic Japanese pattern
- No prior formal communication training

## Available Practice Time
- 15–30 min/day at home
- 15–30 min commuting (train)
