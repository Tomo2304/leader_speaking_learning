# Week 1 — Stop Apologetic Openers
*Phase 1: Foundation | 2026-04-20 to 2026-04-24*

## Key Topic
Eliminating apologetic and hedging language that undermines confidence — replacing "sorry", "I think", "maybe", "I'm not sure but" with direct, assured openers.

## Key Concepts Covered
- Why Japanese communication defaults to apologetic openers and why this reads as low confidence in Western leadership contexts
- The difference between politeness and leadership language
- Replacing hedging with ownership: "My view is...", "My recommendation is...", "Based on what I know..."
