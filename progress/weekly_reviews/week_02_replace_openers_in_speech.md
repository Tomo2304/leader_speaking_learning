# Week 2 — Replace Apologetic Openers in Live Speech
*Phase 1: Foundation | 2026-04-27 to 2026-05-01*

## Key Topic
Taking the habit awareness from Week 1 into live speech — actively replacing apologetic openers in real conversations and meetings in real time.

## Key Concepts Covered
- Recording yourself to hear your actual speech patterns (not what you think you say)
- The replacement drill: "I want to add something here" instead of "Sorry to interrupt"
- Catching the opener before it comes out — the 1-second pause that changes everything

## This Week's Vocabulary Additions

| Original | Improved | Context |
|---|---|---|
| "What do I want to say?" | "What does this audience need from me right now?" | Pre-speaking mindset check — shifts from self-focus to audience-focus |
| "I'll share some information" | "My recommendation is..." | Move from informing to leading — executives expect a position, not a data dump |
| "Goal, problem and solution" | Goal → Problem → Solution (in that order, out loud) | Speaking structure for any update or contribution: start with why it matters, name the obstacle, then give the answer |

---

## Podcast Notes — Speak Up (2026-04-28)

1. **Audience first** — ask "what do they need from this?" before you start speaking, not "what do I want to say?"
2. **Clarity and interest** — strip out the noise, find the compelling angle. One strong point beats three weak ones.
3. **Add a recommendation** — move from informing to leading. Don't just report; take a position.
4. **Your own insight:** speak in Goal → Problem → Solution order. This gives your contribution a narrative spine.
