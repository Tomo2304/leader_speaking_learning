Applying BLUF to spoken communication — leading with your conclusion or recommendation in verbal responses before providing context or detail.

- The discipline of saying your point first, then stopping
- Why over-explaining after the point weakens it
- Verbal BLUF in meetings: "My view is X. Here's why..." not "So what happened was..."
